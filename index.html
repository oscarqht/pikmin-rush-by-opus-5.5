About

Bulborb Rush is a fan-made tower defense tribute in the style of Kingdom Rush, set in the world of Pikmin — but with the roles reversed. You command the native creatures; the Pikmin and their captains are the invaders.

Every sprite, prop, map and sound in this game is generated procedurally in JavaScript at runtime (Canvas 2D + Web Audio). No external images or audio files are used.

Pikmin and all related characters are trademarks of Nintendo. This is a non-commercial fan project.

How to play

  • Click a dirt plot to build a creature. Click a creature to upgrade, evolve at tier 3, or sell it.
  • Press the flashing Onion flag to send the next wave early for bonus pokos.
  • Click the Emperor Bulblax (or his portrait), then click the ground to move him.
  • Snagret Strike (1) and Reinforcements (2) are your special powers.
